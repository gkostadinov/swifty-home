# Code of conduct

Swifty Home is built by people who want their homes to be calmer and safer. We want
the project to feel the same way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. People come from different places and know
  different things.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Make room for newcomers: explain, link, and be patient with first contributions.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Never post someone's personal details, home
  layout, addresses or device identifiers without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or imagery, sexual content, threats,
doxxing, and repeatedly ignoring a request to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, suspend or ban anyone
who breaks this code, in the project's spaces and when representing it elsewhere.
Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting or
by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
